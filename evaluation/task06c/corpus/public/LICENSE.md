# Task 06C corpus licence and provenance

All nine Task 06C evaluation materials are team-authored and released for this evaluation under CC BY 4.0.

- Six entries are byte-for-byte text copies of the frozen Task 06 public corpus so historical and remediation results can be compared without changing the source material.
- Three entries are new Task 06C holdouts and were not used to produce the Task 06 historical metrics.
- No learner data, private reviewer note, copyrighted textbook page, or credential is included.
